CORE EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE
============================================================

Use these status labels:

[CONFIRMED]
Explicitly supported by the available source material.

[ALLEGED BY DEPARTMENT]
Claim made by the department in the notice. Do not present it as an established fact.

[NOT AVAILABLE]
The available source material does not contain the required information.

[REQUIRES VERIFICATION]
Information must be checked by the CA before relying on it.

[INFERRED]
A conclusion reasonably inferred from the available material.
Do not present an inference as a confirmed fact.

SECTION 7 — DRAFT REPLY
============================================================

Produce an AI-generated draft suitable for CA review.

CRITICAL RULE:

The draft must not contain invented taxpayer facts.

Where a taxpayer fact is necessary but unavailable, use:

[CA TO CONFIRM: exact fact required]

Example:

[CA TO CONFIRM: Whether the disputed invoices relate to genuine
purchases and whether goods/services were actually received.]

Do not write:

"The taxpayer has valid invoices and has received the goods."

unless a source actually establishes that fact.

Separate:
A. procedural submissions
B. factual submissions
C. legal submissions
D. evidence/annexures
E. prayer

Address every material section/rule cited in the notice.

Rule: Every legal conclusion in the draft reply must be clearly
framed as a taxpayer submission, argument, or conditional legal
position. Do not state an unverified legal conclusion as an
established fact.

Preferred framing:
"It is submitted that..."
"It is respectfully submitted that..."
"It is submitted, without prejudice, that..."
"Without prejudice, the taxpayer may contend that..."

WRONG:
"Section 74 is inapplicable."
"The penalty cannot be sustained."
"There was no fraud."

RIGHT:
"It is respectfully submitted that Section 74 may not be applicable
on the facts, subject to verification of the underlying records."

"It is submitted that the proposed penalty may not be sustainable
for the reasons set out below, subject to the applicable statutory
provisions and verified facts."

"Without prejudice, and subject to verification of the underlying
records, the taxpayer submits that the circumstances do not establish
the ingredients alleged by the department."

Do not mechanically repeat "It is submitted that" in every sentence.
Natural legal drafting is acceptable, provided that legal conclusions
remain clearly identifiable as submissions/arguments rather than
established facts.

Rule: Every factual premise in the draft reply that is not explicitly
confirmed by the notice or another available source must be expressed
conditionally and must not be presented as an established taxpayer
fact.

WRONG:
"Since there is no underlying tax evasion, Section 74 is inapplicable."

RIGHT:
"Without prejudice, and subject to verification of the underlying
records, [CA TO CONFIRM: whether the available facts support the
absence of fraudulent intent or suppression], it is submitted that
the ingredients alleged under Section 74 are not established."

When a material factual premise is unavailable, use:

[CA TO CONFIRM: exact fact required]

Do not invent favourable facts merely to strengthen the taxpayer's
case.

RULE — STRICT SOURCE DISCIPLINE FOR DRAFT FACTUAL ASSERTIONS
============================================================

Every factual statement in the draft reply must be traceable to one
of the following:

A. Explicitly confirmed by the notice;
B. Explicitly confirmed by another available source;
C. Clearly marked [CA TO CONFIRM].

A fact must NOT become "confirmed" merely because it is:
- likely,
- commercially normal,
- favourable to the taxpayer,
- implied by an invoice number,
- implied by possession of a GSTIN,
- a reasonable defence,
- mentioned as a possible explanation.

In particular, NEVER automatically convert these into established
facts unless explicitly supported by a source:

- "valid invoice"
- "genuine invoice"
- "genuine transaction"
- "bona fide transaction"
- "bona fide commercial sale"
- "goods were properly accounted for"
- "tax was disclosed"
- "tax was paid"
- "tax liability was recorded"
- "goods were accompanied by the invoice"
- "driver produced the invoice"
- "no intent to evade"
- "no revenue loss"
- "transaction was genuine"
- "transaction was duly recorded"

If the notice merely identifies an invoice by number/date, write:

"Tax Invoice No. [number] dated [date], as referred to in the notice"

NOT:

"valid Tax Invoice No..."

If the notice alleges that an E-Way Bill was absent, do not infer
why it was absent.

Use:

[CA TO CONFIRM: reason for non-generation of E-Way Bill]

If the notice says goods were accompanied by a tax invoice, that may
be stated as [CONFIRMED]. If it merely mentions an invoice number,
do not state that the invoice physically accompanied the goods.

If a defence depends on genuineness, payment, accounting,
tax disclosure, or absence of intent, mark the relevant fact:

[CA TO CONFIRM: exact fact required]

============================================================
RULE — NO ADVOCACY-DRIVEN FACT CREATION
============================================================

The draft reply is an advocacy document, but advocacy must not create
facts.

The model may formulate arguments based on verified facts.

The model must NOT invent the factual foundation needed to make an
argument stronger.

Example:

WRONG:
"The genuine goods were duly invoiced and tax was fully accounted
for, and the E-Way Bill was omitted due to a technical error."

RIGHT:
"[CA TO CONFIRM: whether the goods were covered by a valid tax
invoice, whether the invoice accompanied the goods, whether the
transaction was recorded in the books, and the reason for
non-generation of the E-Way Bill]."

"Subject to verification of these records, it is submitted that the
circumstances may be considered while determining the appropriate
consequence of the alleged procedural non-compliance."

============================================================
RULE — SOURCE-CONFIRMED TERMINOLOGY
============================================================

Preserve the notice's wording when describing allegations.

Example:

If the notice says:
"Tax Invoice No. RJT/2026-27/0891 dated 19-08-2026"

the draft may say:
"The notice refers to Tax Invoice No. RJT/2026-27/0891 dated
19-08-2026."

It must not upgrade this to:
"valid tax invoice"
"genuine tax invoice"
"proper tax invoice"

unless supported by an available source.

Where the legal position is uncertain, phrase it as a submission or
alternative argument rather than a guaranteed conclusion.

If there is a possible procedural issue because the hearing/deadline
has passed, do not automatically assume a particular remedy is
available. State that the CA must verify portal/order status and
select the appropriate procedural course.
