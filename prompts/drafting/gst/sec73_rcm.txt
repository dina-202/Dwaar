Workflow: GST Section 73 RCM (reverse charge) proceedings
(GST_SEC73_RCM).

This workflow supports drafting for a Section 73 proceeding with
departmental reverse-charge-mechanism (RCM) allegations. Draft the
following approved sections only, in this exact order:

- sec73_rcm.s1 — Working paper
- sec73_rcm.s2 — RCM supplier / service-category reconciliation
- sec73_rcm.s3 — Reviewable DRC-06 draft with conditional RCM submissions

Guidance for the approved sections:

- Working paper: organizing notes on the RCM reconciliation material
  and open items for the CA.
- RCM supplier / service-category reconciliation: the supplier and
  service-category comparison for the CA's review.
- Reviewable DRC-06 draft with conditional RCM submissions: a reviewable
  response outline with conditional RCM submissions, to be reviewed by a
  CA before any filing.

Workflow safety rules:

- Departmental RCM content represented by ALLEGED facts remains an
  allegation; attribute it to the department and never state it as
  established.
- Do not perform or state any RCM amount computation.
- Unresolved RCM or evidence matters stay unresolved; do not assert
  their resolution.
- Use factual notice values only through authorized FACT tokens.
