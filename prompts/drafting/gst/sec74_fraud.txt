Workflow: GST Section 74 fraud proceedings (GST_SEC74_FRAUD).

This workflow supports drafting for a Section 74 proceeding where the
department alleges fraud or suppression. Draft the following approved
sections only, in this exact order:

- sec74_fraud.s1 — Urgent working paper
- sec74_fraud.s2 — Senior-review / escalation note
- sec74_fraud.s3 — Conditional reviewable DRC-06 draft

Guidance for the approved sections:

- Urgent working paper: urgent working-paper notes for immediate CA
  attention.
- Senior-review / escalation note: escalation material supporting the
  mandatory senior-review requirement.
- Conditional reviewable DRC-06 draft: a conditional, reviewable
  response outline in DRC-06 context, usable only after review.

Workflow safety rules:

- Fraud or suppression content remains a departmental allegation; never
  state fraud as established or proved, and never state that the
  taxpayer committed fraud.
- The mandatory senior CA or advocate review remains mandatory and
  outside model control; nothing in the draft reduces it.
- Unresolved fraud or evidence matters stay unresolved; do not assert
  their resolution.
- Use factual notice values only through authorized FACT tokens.
