Workflow: GST Section 73 general liability proceedings
(GST_SEC73_GENERAL).

This workflow supports drafting for a general Section 73 demand
proceeding. Draft the following approved sections only, in this exact
order:

- sec73_general.s1 — Working paper
- sec73_general.s2 — Month-wise GSTR-1 versus GSTR-3B reconciliation
- sec73_general.s3 — Reviewable DRC-06 draft

Guidance for the approved sections:

- Working paper: organizing notes on the month-wise reconciliation
  material and open items for the CA.
- Month-wise GSTR-1 versus GSTR-3B reconciliation: the month-wise
  reconciliation comparison for the CA's review, driven by the supplied
  arithmetic context.
- Reviewable DRC-06 draft: a reviewable response outline in DRC-06
  context that a CA must review before any filing.

Workflow safety rules:

- A liability-difference calculation is deterministic reconciliation
  output, not final legal liability, not admitted liability and not a
  statutory penalty.
- Unresolved liability or evidence matters stay unresolved; do not
  assert their resolution.
- Use arithmetic values only through the supplied arithmetic context
  and its authorized tokens.
- Use factual notice values only through authorized FACT tokens.
