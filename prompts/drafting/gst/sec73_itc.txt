Workflow: GST Section 73 ITC proceedings (GST_SEC73_ITC).

This workflow supports drafting for a Section 73 ITC-mismatch
proceeding. Draft the following approved sections only, in this exact
order:

- sec73_itc.s1 — Working paper
- sec73_itc.s2 — ITC reconciliation table
- sec73_itc.s3 — Reviewable DRC-06 draft

Guidance for the approved sections:

- Working paper: organizing notes on the ITC reconciliation material
  and open items for the CA.
- ITC reconciliation table: the ITC reconciliation comparison for the
  CA's review, driven by the supplied arithmetic context.
- Reviewable DRC-06 draft: a reviewable response outline in DRC-06
  context that a CA must review before any filing.

Workflow safety rules:

- An ITC reconciliation difference is deterministic reconciliation
  output, not final legal liability, not admitted liability and not a
  statutory penalty.
- Unresolved ITC or evidence matters stay unresolved; do not assert
  their resolution.
- Use arithmetic values only through the supplied arithmetic context
  and its authorized tokens.
- Use factual notice values only through authorized FACT tokens.
