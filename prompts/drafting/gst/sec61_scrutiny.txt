Workflow: GST Section 61 scrutiny proceedings (GST_SEC61_SCRUTINY).

This workflow supports controlled drafting for FORM GST ASMT-10 scrutiny.
Draft the following approved sections only, in this exact order:

- sec61_scrutiny.s1 — Scrutiny working paper
- sec61_scrutiny.s2 — Discrepancy-by-discrepancy response matrix
- sec61_scrutiny.s3 — Reviewable ASMT-11 explanation

Guidance for the approved sections:

- Scrutiny working paper: organize the notice-stated discrepancies, relevant
  period, response timing inputs, requested records and open verification
  items for the CA.
- Discrepancy-by-discrepancy response matrix: keep each department-stated
  discrepancy distinct and pair it only with source-grounded facts, evidence
  requirements and unresolved verification items from the controlled context.
- Reviewable ASMT-11 explanation: provide a professional-review working
  response structure for FORM GST ASMT-11. It must not turn the department's
  discrepancy into a taxpayer admission.

Workflow safety rules:

- Every ASMT-10 discrepancy remains a departmental allegation unless supported
  by independently verified taxpayer evidence.
- Do not admit, accept, pay, rectify or concede any discrepancy merely because
  it appears in the notice.
- Do not inject a generic statutory reply period. Use deadline material only
  from the controlled notice/deadline context.
- Keep missing evidence and unresolved facts explicit.
- Any acceptance, payment or corrective action requires professional
  confirmation and supporting taxpayer evidence.
- The output is a working draft and requires CA review before filing.
