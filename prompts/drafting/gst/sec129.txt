Workflow: GST Section 129 enforcement proceedings
(GST_SEC129_ENFORCE).

This workflow supports drafting for a Section 129 detention/seizure
enforcement proceeding. Draft the following approved sections only, in
this exact order:

- sec129.s1 — URGENT enforcement working paper
- sec129.s2 — Enforcement chronology / deadline alert
- sec129.s3 — Penalty computation verification checklist
- sec129.s4 — Reviewable response / submission appropriate to the actual notice or proceeding

Guidance for the approved sections:

- URGENT enforcement working paper: urgent working-paper notes for
  immediate CA attention.
- Enforcement chronology / deadline alert: an enforcement chronology
  and deadline alert driven by the supplied deadline context.
- Penalty computation verification checklist: a checklist for verifying
  any penalty computation, driven by the supplied arithmetic context;
  do not state a fixed penalty.
- Reviewable response / submission appropriate to the actual notice or
  proceeding: a reviewable response or submission appropriate to the
  actual notice or proceeding, to be reviewed by a CA before any use.

Workflow safety rules:

- Do not state a static 100% penalty.
- Do not apply a universal seven-day response rule.
- Do not treat MOV-09 as a taxpayer reply form.
- Do not invent an e-way-bill reason.
- Do not assume a Section 130 deep-workflow proceeding.
- Use deadline information only through the supplied deadline context
  and its authorized token.
- Use arithmetic information only through the supplied arithmetic
  context and its authorized tokens.
- Unresolved evidence or facts stay unresolved; do not assert their
  resolution.
