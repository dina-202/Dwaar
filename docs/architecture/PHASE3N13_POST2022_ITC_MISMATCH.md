# Phase 3N.13 — Post-2022 ITC Mismatch Regime

**Status:** implementation slice  
**Date:** 23 September 2026  
**Depends on:** Phase 3N.12 verified historical ITC mismatch regimes

## Goal

Extend the narrow `ITC_MISMATCH_VERIFICATION` research topic across the 1 January 2022 legal boundary without conflating mismatch treatment with final ITC eligibility.

## Official authority

Circular No. 193/05/2023-GST records that, with effect from 1 January 2022, consequent to insertion of clause (aa) in section 16(2) and amendment of rule 36(4), ITC in respect of a supply is not to be allowed unless the supplier has reported it in FORM GSTR-1 or IFF and the supply is communicated to the recipient in FORM GSTR-2B.

The source remains the official GST Council copy of the CBIC circular already curated in Phase 3N.12.

## Catalog version

The GST legal catalog advances to:

`gst-legal.v4.2026-09-23`

Historical saved legal briefs remain immutable and retain their prior catalog version.

## Semantic version chain

The semantic key remains:

`cbic.itc_mismatch_verification`

Its closed source-verified versions are now:

- v1 — Circular 183 scope: FY 2017-18 and FY 2018-19;
- v2 — Circular 193 historical 2A / Rule 36(4) guidance: 1 April 2019 through 31 December 2021;
- v3 — Circular 193 post-2022 supplier-reporting / GSTR-2B communication rule: 1 January 2022 onward.

The versions do not overlap.

## Full-period safety

Phase 3N.11 still governs selection.

Therefore:

- FY 2022-23 can resolve the post-2022 version;
- an explicit 1 January 2022–31 March 2022 source period can resolve it;
- FY 2021-22 remains unresolved as one broad period because it crosses the 1 January 2022 boundary.

Dwaar does not stitch the pre-2022 and post-2022 versions together to make FY 2021-22 appear uniform.

## Important boundary

This phase does **not** mark `ITC_ELIGIBILITY` as resolved.

The post-2022 supplier-reporting / GSTR-2B communication condition is one legal condition in the mismatch context. Final ITC entitlement can still depend on other statutory facts and restrictions.

## Safety invariant

> A verified GSTR-2B communication requirement is not the same as a verified case-specific conclusion that ITC is eligible or ineligible.
